## Audio Tips

- Speak slowly — 2 min feels longer than you think
- Pause 1-2 seconds between sections
- Record in a quiet room (closet with clothes works well)
- Do NOT record midday when you might be tired
- If you flub a line, just pause 2 seconds and restart the sentence — easy to edit out
